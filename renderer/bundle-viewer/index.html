Provenance

No basemap. This bundle renders in its own projected CRS with no reprojection, and basemap tiles are Web Mercator — showing one would mean either reprojecting the data, which this bundle deliberately does not do, or drawing two coordinate systems on one canvas. This is the recorded consequence of the publishing path, not a missing feature.
Verified before drawing: the manifest's version and asset paths; the style's content hash and version; and for every partition its byte count, content hash, row count, coordinate-space envelope and declared attribute columns.
Carried but not checked: the identity facts above are the publisher's claims, shown as claims — nothing here re-verifies uniqueness. Attribute presence is checked; Arrow types are not. And this page cannot verify the code that is already running: the manifest's viewer-asset hashes are for an external verifier, and the chain of trust does not close inside the browser.
