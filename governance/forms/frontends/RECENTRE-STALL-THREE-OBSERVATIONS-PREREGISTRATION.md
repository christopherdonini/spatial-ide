## §5 Predictions, unchanged items, invalidators, falsification

These are written before any run. A wrong prediction is a result, and no prediction is edited.

**Terms.**
- **R-span:** the span of the render that re-bases. Which type applies comes from the step's node mark, never from the line pattern.
  - For a drag, a wheel tick or a double-click: the first span line after the last `view-state` line that still shows the old origin. That line is traced before `maybeRecenter` in the same callback (`WorkingCanvas.tsx`, lines 2161, 2192 and 2197 at cdfebef1). At deck 9.3.9 every tick of a double-click starts or lies inside the controller's 300 ms transition, so no double-click tick re-centres (lines 2150 to 2152 and 2192 at cdfebef1; deck's `dist/controllers/controller.js`, line 538, `dist/controllers/orthographic-controller.js`, lines 316 to 319, `dist/controllers/transition-manager.js`, lines 136 to 146, and `dist/transitions/transition.js`, lines 77 to 81, which sends the last update before it ends the transition, package-relative). A re-centre that is due comes at the next tick with neither, which is a later step's, and belongs to that step. The double-click rule covers a deck that behaves otherwise.
  - For a fit, case 3, or the DEV seam in run D: the first span line after the first `view-state` line that shows the new origin (lines 1439 to 1448 and 2404 to 2410 at cdfebef1).
- **No R-span:** a step whose `view-state` lines show no origin change has none (in run D, a seam call to the origin already set).
- **Long gap:** a kept gap of at least `LONG_GAP_MS`.
- **Baseline:** the median of the same run's spans that are not R-spans, counting the span after the open's fit `view-state` line as an R-span, and whose `totalPositions` is within 5% of the R-span's. With fewer than three such spans, O1 is not read at that R-span, and that is recorded.

**5.1 The three observations,** each read per run on the 100k fixture.

**O1, the timer.** Read at each R-span of a re-centring drag, case 2's wheel tick, case 3, and run D's re-centring drags and seam calls.
- Confirms candidate 1's shell part: the R-span is at least 100 ms and at least 2.0 times the baseline.
- Kills that part: the R-span is below 1.5 times the baseline.
- Otherwise: neither.
- Recorded beside it: the R-span's share of the kept gap that holds it.

**O2, the counter.** Read over the first two metrics windows whose page times follow an R-span's t1, their counts and times summed. Two windows are read because deck emits a window at the start of a frame, before that frame's layer update (S0 records the lines), so the first window can close before deck has updated the R-span's layers.
- **The interval** runs from the second-last metrics line before the R-span's t1 (or the run's start) to the second window's page time. deck resets its counts when it emits a window, before that frame's layer update, so the first window can hold the updates of layers set just before the last metrics line before t1; and a later render before deck's next frame replaces the R-span's layers (S0 records the lines).
- Confirms the trigger: the summed `updateAttributesCount` (by S0's reading) is at least the R-span's `layerCount`, no other `layers` line lies in the interval, and, over every pair of consecutive windows (each window with the next) with no `layers` line in either, the median summed count is less than one.
- Kills the trigger: the summed count is below the least `layerCount` of the R-span's `layers` line and every other `layers` line in the interval.
- Otherwise: neither, including a run with no pair for the control. Where another `layers` line lies in the interval, so that another render's attribute updates may be in the sum or may stand in for the R-span's, each such line's `layerCount` is recorded.
- When the trigger holds and a long gap ends inside those two windows, their summed `updateAttributesTime` reads one of three ways:
  - at least 50% of that gap: the multiplier lies inside deck's attribute timer. That timer covers the attribute updaters and the buffer writes they make (S0 records it), so this supports candidate 1's deck part and does not exclude a wait on the GPU process during those writes;
  - at most 10%: the multiplier lies outside that timer (candidate 2 or 3 away from those writes, or deck work the timer does not cover, as S0 records);
  - between: neither.

**O3, the line against the gaps.** Read per long gap. An R-span is near a long gap when its t1 lies inside the gap or no more than `ADJACENT_MS` before the gap's start, or when it lies inside a kept gap that ends no more than `ADJACENT_MS` before the long gap's start. Near is defined this way because deck queues the layers in `setProps` and updates them in its next frame (S0 records the lines), so a re-centre can end its R-span before the gap opens.
- Confirms, for that gap: an R-span with `totalPositions` of at least 1,800,000 is near it.
- Kills candidate 1 as that gap's cause: no R-span is near it. That gap is listed with its nearest step marks.
- Otherwise (only R-spans with `totalPositions` below 1,800,000 are near it): neither, recorded with each near R-span's `totalPositions`.

**A run that matches neither.**
- No long gap in the run: on that build, suite and run, the re-centre did not stall for `LONG_GAP_MS`. O1 and O2 are still read.
- No long gap in any measure-build run, but long gaps under run D: the stall was seen on run D and not on M1 to M3. Run D differs from them both in its build (dev) and in its suite (pan-anchor's cases and its DEV-only seam), and these runs do not separate the two.
- Mixed results: each gap is read on its own, and candidate 1 holds only for the gaps it explains.
