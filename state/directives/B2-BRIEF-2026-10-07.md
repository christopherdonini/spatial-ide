# B2 brief — Save project, Snapshot data, session history, lineage and preferences (2026-10-07)

*Written by the main architect. It replaces §10 of `SHELL-MIGRATION-PLAN-2026-10-07.md` (second version) as the brief for B2's forms, and it carries the human's rulings of 2026-10-07 on that section's questions. The rest of that plan stands unchanged. Below, "the plan" is that file, and "the decisions" is the section "Chris's decisions after the walkthrough" of `O-07-WALKTHROUGH-2026-10-05.md`. §9's split of stage 1 and its arrangement of stages 2 and 3 are proposals until the human rules them.*

## 1. What changed against the plan's §10

- **Session history** is kept on the machine and found by the project's identity. After a crash, the next open offers to recover it.
- **Saving mid-session** changes nothing the user sees. A "saved here" mark is added.
- **Steps left behind after a save** leave the lineage at the next Save project. The lineage file is therefore rewritten to match the saved project, and not only appended to.
- **No kept branches,** in either form.
- **An export** keeps its own copy of the steps it was made from.
- **Reproducibility:** the data's grade stays, with a "recipe incomplete" marker beside it. ADR-005 needs no amendment (§8).
- **Stage 1** becomes three pieces, and stage 3 becomes two (§9).
- **The docs/07 note** lands with the first piece that records a step, or just before it (§11).

## 2. What the user gets

- **Save project,** with a data choice:
  - **Keep linked to the live file** (the default): no whole-file scan. Reopening shows the file as it is now, and the app says if it changed.
  - **Freeze a snapshot:** takes time and disk space, says so first, and can be cancelled. Reopening shows the same data every time.
- **Snapshot data on its own,** without saving the project.
- **Reopen.**
- **Session history:** every committed step of this session, dead ends included, with restore to any of them and a "saved here" mark.
- **Recovery after a crash:** the next open offers the unsaved steps.
- **Lineage:** every step that contributes to the project as saved, kept in the project, with restore to its earlier steps.
- **Three views of the lineage:** Data lineage, Map recipe and Sources. Past exports are linked from them.
- **Sharing levels** for the lineage, and **settings** for what is tracked.
- **Preferences in two layers.**
- **A one-line purpose at the top of each of the three dialogs,** and a plain explanation of "Reference" where that state is shown.

## 3. Six things, stored apart

| Thing | Holds | Stored | Travels with a shared project |
|---|---|---|---|
| Project | the data link or snapshot reference, filters, style, map view, selection rules, and the analysis settings (units, CRS, styles) | the project file | yes |
| Snapshot | a sealed copy of the data | the app's protected storage | only when the user chooses |
| Session history | every committed step of the current session, dead ends included | the machine, found by the project's identity and not by its path | never |
| Lineage | every contributing step, data and style, from the raw data to the project as saved | the project's hidden folder | at the level the user chooses |
| Preferences | panel layout, personal settings and the tracking settings | the machine | never |
| Exported map | a finished map for others | where the user exports it | it is what is shared |

## 4. The model: one record of steps, in two layers

- **A step is a committed change:** to data, scope or style, a snapshot, or an export.
- **Never a step:** zoom, pan, panel layout and preferences. A selection by itself is not a step either; a step that uses one records it as its scope.
- **Session history** keeps every step as the user works, with its dead ends.
  - It is written as the user works, so a crash loses nothing.
  - After a crash, the next open offers to recover the unsaved steps. Recovered steps enter the lineage only at Save project. If the user declines, they are cleared.
  - It is cleared when the session ends. A crash is not the end of a session.
  - Saving mid-session changes nothing in it. A "saved here" mark shows which steps are already in the lineage and which are new since the last save.
- **Lineage** keeps the steps that contribute to the project as saved. At each Save project:
  - the new steps that survive are added. Dead ends, reversals, clause toggles and repeated edits of one property collapse, so five colour tries leave the last one. Distinct steps that each contribute stay;
  - saved steps that the user has since left behind, by going back to an earlier step and continuing from there, leave it. They stay in the session history until the session ends.
- **The lineage always matches the project as saved.** Closing without saving changes nothing in it, and no save empties it.
- **No kept branches in B2,** neither one branch nor every branch. The step record keeps its parent field for later.
- **An export made from steps that are later left behind** keeps its own copy of those steps with its audit record (§8).
- **Snapshots are anchor steps.** The first one pins the raw data.
- **Restore** is explicit: to any step of the current session, and to any lineage step from earlier sessions.
- **The honest limit:** a step that depends on data which has changed since cannot be restored exactly, and the app says so. With a snapshot, every step stays restorable.

## 5. What every step records

One line of plain text per step, in a versioned format (docs/02, "Projects": plain-text, diffable files):

- **Intent:** the typed action and its typed parameters, with units.
- **Kind:** data, scope, style, snapshot or export.
- **Dependencies:** the fields, the CRS and the kind of data the step relies on.
- **Data identity:** the snapshot's fingerprint, or the live file's change-detection observation, through a lasting dataset reference and never a session handle.
- **Parent.** B2 keeps no branch, and the field stays.
- **Actor:** the user, an AI or a plugin. It is recorded from B2 on, although B2 shows only the user.
- **Effect class,** by ADR-006. Today's filter, scope and style steps are workspace mutations, and an export is an external side effect.
- **Links** to related records: an export's audit record and a snapshot's identity.

The fields are fixed once, in piece 1a's form. The list of actions grows with the features, by version.

## 6. Views, sharing and settings

- **Views in B2:** the session history list with its "saved here" mark, Data lineage (data and scope steps), Map recipe (every contributing step), and Sources (which datasets, versions and snapshots went in, with attribution and licence where the file states them).
- **Later views** (parameters, notes, AI-made steps, quality, edits) arrive with their features. The step record already carries what they need.
- **Sharing a project,** the user chooses: no lineage, the full lineage, or the lineage with chosen steps withheld.
- **Tracking settings,** in the two layers of preferences (the user's defaults, plus a per-project override):
  - record lineage for this project, on or off. When off, the project says "lineage not recorded";
  - which kinds are recorded. Data and scope steps are always recorded while lineage is on;
  - the default sharing level.
- **What an incomplete lineage shows** (the human's lean of 2026-10-07): the data's reproducibility grade stays as it is, and a marker beside it says "recipe incomplete".
  - With steps withheld, the marker gives their number.
  - With a kind turned off, the steps were never recorded, so there is no number. The marker names the kind.
  - A project saved before lineage existed, or with lineage turned on later, says where its lineage starts.
- **Always on:** an AI-made step is always labelled.
- **The firm line:** no setting and no edit removes a step that shaped the result while the lineage still claims to be complete. Every reduction shows as a marker (docs/01, principle 8).
- **Organising is free:** naming steps, adding notes, grouping and collapsing on screen.

## 7. Preferences

- The user's defaults apply to every project.
- "Use a custom setup for this project" overrides only what the user changes. It is stored on the machine, found by the project's identity, and is never written into the project file.
- They hold milestone 1's layout state and §6's tracking settings.

## 8. What the architecture requires of B2

These follow from the constitution and the code. They are requirements on B2's forms, not new decisions.

- **The record is the kernel's, not the shell's.** docs/02 says undo is "never implemented in the UI" and that workspace mutations go through a kernel command and event log. An actor other than the user can only be recorded if steps pass through the public interface (docs/01, principles 4 and 5).
  - So the changes B2 records (filter, scope, style) become kernel commands, and the protocol gains them.
  - Milestone 2's typed transitions are their shell-side shape (the plan, §6.5).
  - Persisting a style triggers ADR-006's class-2 obligations and docs/11's, which ADR-022 left "not granted". Piece 1b's form asks for that grant.
- **Names against ADR-006.** The user-facing "Lineage" is wider than ADR-006's "lineage DAG". The forms state the mapping once, and code and records keep ADR-006's terms:

  | The user sees | ADR-006 class | Machinery |
  |---|---|---|
  | filter, scope and style steps | workspace mutations | the kernel's command and event log |
  | export steps | external side effects | the audit log, linked from the step |
  | processing steps (none exist yet) | pure transformations | the lineage DAG |

  - No code or record calls the step file a lineage DAG.
  - "Replay" stays reserved for pure transformations. B2's restore is a restore of workspace state.
- **The project's identity** is ADR-005's stable URI for project state, kept apart from where the folder sits. It is created at the first Save project and written in the project file. The session history and the per-project preferences are found by it.
- **A lasting dataset reference** (docs/11's ResourceRef) is a prerequisite. Every handle is session-scoped today (SKP-V0 §3).
  - A linked file's reference has no content hash until a snapshot gives one. Its source revision is today's change-detection observation.
  - So, as far as this brief can see, piece 1a needs no whole-file scan and does not wait for the ADR-029 ruling. Its form confirms that.
- **A recorded selection** can be re-established only where feature identity is stable (docs/11, "Stable feature IDs"): a declared identity column and unchanged data. Otherwise the step says that its selection could not be restored.
- **A durable format needs an ADR.** The project folder's layout and the step record are read by later versions and by other people. Piece 1a's form proposes the ADR, and the human accepts it before piece 1c merges, the first piece that writes a file others will read.
- **The reproducibility display needs no ADR-005 amendment.**
  - ADR-005 grades the inputs: "a derived output's grade is the weakest among its inputs". It says nothing about how complete the recipe is.
  - The marker of §6 is a second statement beside the grade. The five levels and the weakest-input rule stay as written. The format ADR defines the marker as a property of the lineage record.
  - Best-effort would be the wrong level: ADR-005 defines it as "nondeterministic or external", and showing it would hide the data's real grade.
  - **One condition keeps the grade true:** the saved project alone must be enough to get the result again. In B2 it always is, because the project file holds the final filters and style. When processing steps exist, a withheld step whose output is not kept would break that, and the rule for that case is written then, in docs/11.
- **The export's copy of its steps, against today's audit log.** The audit log is per user, on the machine, outside the project and the bundle, append-only, with a size ceiling and rotation (`kernel/src/permission/audit/`). `kernel/README.md` says it is not the class-2 log.
  - So the copy never travels with a shared project, which fits the sharing levels.
  - It lasts as long as the audit log keeps the record.
  - It needs a second version of the audit schema. I recommend the steps go in a file beside the log, with their digest in the record, because the log's write guarantee assumes small records.
- **docs/07 gets a dated note** (§11).

## 9. Stages and pieces

B2 keeps its five stages. Stage 1 holds four separable things: a design, a reference, recording and saving. I recommend three pieces for it, and two for stage 3.

| Piece | Holds | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| **1a** | The design: the full step record and the format ADR, as Proposed. The code: the lasting dataset reference. | no shell milestone |
| **1b** | Recording: the kernel's step commands for filter, scope and style, and the session history on the machine, safe against a crash. The docs/07 note. | 1a, and milestone 2 |
| **1c** | Save project (linked) and reopen: the project's identity, the project file, the notice that a file changed, the offer to recover after a crash, and the "saved here" mark. | 1b, and the format ADR accepted |
| **2** | The lineage file: what Save project adds, collapses and removes. The export's copy of its steps. | 1c |
| **3a** | Snapshot data on its own: the sealed copy, its fingerprint, progress and cancel, and the snapshot step in the session history. | 1b, and the ADR-029 ruling |
| **3b** | "Freeze a snapshot" inside Save project, the anchor step in the lineage, and the rebind to a new generation. | 2 and 3a |
| **4** | The session history list, the three views, and restore. | 3b |
| **5** | Sharing levels, tracking settings and the two-layer preferences. | 4 |

- **Why stage 1 splits.**
  - The design is reviewed before any code depends on it, and before milestone 2's form, whose typed transitions are shaped to become these commands.
  - The reference needs no shell work, so it can start early on the data lane.
  - Recording needs milestone 2, because scope exists only from there.
  - Saving needs recording, because persisting a style brings ADR-006's class-2 obligations.
- **Piece 1a's form is stage 1's form.** It fixes the full step record and proposes the format ADR. Pieces 1b and 1c cite it, and neither changes the record or the ADR without the human's word.
- **Stages 2 and 3 can run side by side only in part.** They meet in two places: the Save project flow, and the anchor step in the lineage. Piece 3a touches neither, so it can run beside stage 2. Piece 3b touches both, so it follows.
  - Running 2 and 3a together holds both slots.
  - Piece 3a waits for the ADR-029 ruling and stage 2 does not, so in practice stage 2 can start first.
- **Between 1c and 2,** a saved project has no lineage yet. Its lineage starts at its first save after stage 2, and says so (§6).
- **Screens** need milestones 2 and 5, as the plan says (§4).

## 11. The docs/07 note, proposed wording

For the human to type, under Alpha's "Notebooks: record and replay (03)", on the pattern of the 2026-08-18 Action console note. It lands with piece 1b, the first piece that records a step, or in its own commit just before.

> *(Appended, with its date.)* **Split.** The Prototype's B2 ships **recording only**: the kernel records every committed change to a project's data, scope or style as a typed step (ADR-006, workspace mutations), keeps the steps for the session, and at Save project keeps the contributing ones in the project's lineage file. A project can be restored to a recorded step. This is not the Alpha item: replaying recorded steps, notebooks and workflows (13's Workflow IR) **stay Alpha**. It is not the Beta item either: a restore re-derives no data and compares no branches, B2 keeps no branch, and **lineage time travel and scenario branches stay Beta**.

## 12. Earlier records, and what is not in B2

- **"History stays session-only for B2"** (`LAYER-MODEL-DECISIONS-2026-09-28.md` §2, last row, and the design notebook §3.5) is replaced: the session history is still cleared with the session, and the lineage is saved.
- **"Freeze a snapshot" inside Save project** is save, prepare and rebind in one flow for the user. Internally the three stay distinct, and the 2026-09-18 direction holds: save performs no whole-file scan, and prepare is explicit and cancellable.
- **B2 stays a single-dataset project.**
- **The plan's line "the ADR-029 scan-progress ruling before B2's backend stages"** narrows to piece 3a, if 1a's form confirms §8.
- **Not in B2:** workflows, notebooks, replay on new data, export from a saved project, CLI replay, kept branches, the later views of §6, and projects with several datasets.
