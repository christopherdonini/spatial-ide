## 1. Definitions

- **Piece**: one `node` in the gate log (190). A node can have several counts (a fresh count restarts its attempt numbers) and several PRs; it is measured as one piece.
- **First-pass result per gate**: the verdict of the piece's first gate-log row for that gate role (reviewer, architect, tester). *Passed* means PASS or PASS WITH NOTES. A first row with an attempt number above 1 (a gate added mid-piece) is shown as `F@a2` and so on.
- **Correction round**: a round k ≥ 2 of a count, entered after a FAIL at round k−1 of the same count (any gate). A re-gate that follows only passes (for example a re-check ordered by a ruling) is not a correction round. *Record rounds* are the correction rounds whose rows the log marks as record rounds (`record: true`, or "record round" in the note). *Counts* is the number of fresh counts the node went through.
- **Finding classes**: Correctness (C), Evidence (E), Documentation (D), per AUTONOMY §22. A finding is an item a report raises as blocking, must-fix or should-fix; notes, nits, conditions before the merge and closed earlier findings are not counted. *Blocking* counts the findings that failed a gate, of any class. From 2026-10-05 (the proportional-gates direction) most reports sort their findings by class; before it they mostly sort by severity (B, S1, S2, F, N), so a September class is usually **inferred** by a keyword rule (section 2 gives its measured agreement).
- **Days from form commit to merge**: from the committer time of the commit that first added the piece's form (followed across the piece F move) to the committer time of the merge commit of the latest merged PR that the piece's gate rows or its PLAN node name. Pieces with no form, or no merged PR, have no figure.
- **Piece size**: the line budget the form declares (a five-line form's Scope line; a full form's §7: its budget table's total row, else its first line-budget phrase, else the table's row sum). It is the declared figure, not the final diff.
- **Month**: the month of the piece's first gate row. **Lane**: the node's PLAN lane; 16 gated nodes are not PLAN nodes. **Agent role**: the gate role that wrote the report (section 7a) and the role that drafted the form, as its header states (section 7b).

## Appendix C. Method and limits

- **Finding extraction** (`scripts/parse-report.mjs`) reads the labels the gates write (C1, E-1, D-open-1, B1, S1-1, S2-1, F1, G2-1, N1 and other short bold labels under a findings heading). Each label counts once per report. Items that only report a passing check or close an earlier finding are skipped. The logged verdict is authoritative: an item that looks blocking in a report whose verdict passed is counted as a note; in a FAIL report with no blocking item read, labelled items under plain headings, then list items under a Correctness, Evidence or blocking heading, then the verdict line's class words stand in. Section 2 gives the hand checks and their outcome, including the fixes they caused.
- **Classes before 2026-10-05** are mostly inferred (`classify` in `scripts/parse-report.mjs`): an untrue or unsupported claim is C, then a missing run, test or observation is E, then words, records and cites are D, else C. Its agreement with stated classes is in section 2; E is the weakest class. Before 2026-10-05 a Documentation or record finding could fail a gate, so September D findings include blocking ones.
- **Rows without reports** (before AUTONOMY §25) count for first-pass results, correction rounds and days, never for classes or rules.
- **Counts and correction rounds** are read from attempt numbers in `state/gate-log.json`. A count restarts when a gate's attempt number does not rise; attempt numbering is the custodian's, and a few nodes number per PR or per generation.
- **Days** use committer times. A form committed on main before its branch, or a piece merged in several PRs, is measured to the last merge. Forms shared by several nodes (the ADMISSION and B1 forms) give each node the same start, and a node whose PLAN `gate` names a later generation's form (for example `geometry-types-beyond-polygons`, whose gate rows begin with ADR-034 and whose PLAN gate is the MP-1 form) is measured from that form.
- **Declared lines** are the form's own figure as first declared; amendments (class 6 or 8 overruns) are not read. Forms that declare no line figure, and the 31 pieces with no form, are shown as their own groups.
- **Rules**: the catalogue holds the rules a gate can find broken in a piece; rules addressed only to the gates themselves (the report's first line, the replacement-words procedure of AUTONOMY §31) or only to the custodian's operations are left out. A finding can match several rules, and 58% match none. The keyword tier misses findings that describe a breach in other words (section 2, the unmatched sample), and its precision varies by rule (section 8).
- **Nothing here is a verdict on a piece, a gate or a rule.** The numbers describe the records as they stand at the measured commit.
