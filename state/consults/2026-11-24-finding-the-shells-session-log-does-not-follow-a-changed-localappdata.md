- **What.** The app takes its store from the `LOCALAPPDATA` variable (the header of `frontends/shell/e2e/recording.mjs` names `kernel/src/permission/audit/log.rs` and its `data_dir`), but it writes its session log through Tauri's `app_log_dir()` (`frontends/shell/src-tauri/src/lib.rs`). So a run with that variable set leaves its session log outside its scratch folder.
