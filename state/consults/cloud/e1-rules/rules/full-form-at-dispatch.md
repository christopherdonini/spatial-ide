# full-form-at-dispatch

*Paraphrase, not the human's words: the binding text is at the source.*

When the Out-of-scope line a piece would carry at dispatch names a full-gating category as touched (anything short of asserting it touches none), the piece takes the full form and full gating from dispatch, and no five-line form is committed for it. A category first found mid-piece follows single-gate-route, and a committed five-line form is not rewritten. A filler piece sets this rule aside (filler-slot-admission).
