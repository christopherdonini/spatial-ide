# filler-slot-conduct

*Paraphrase, not the human's words: the binding text is at the source.*

A filler's heavy runs yield to slots 1 and 2: shared holds only, never an exclusive hold while a slot 1 or 2 piece has runs pending. A question for the human is asked when it arises, never held until a filler finishes. A resuming slot 1 or 2 piece never stops the filler in flight. The weekly window reports the fillers merged and the hours slots 1 and 2 spent waiting.
