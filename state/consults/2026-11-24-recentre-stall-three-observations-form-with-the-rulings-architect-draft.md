## §5 Predictions, unchanged items, invalidators, falsification

These are written before any run. A wrong prediction is a result, and no prediction is edited.

**Terms.**
- **R-span:** the span of the render that re-bases. Which type applies comes from the step's node mark, never from the line pattern.
  - For a drag or a wheel tick: the first span line after the last `view-state` line that still shows the old origin. That line is traced before `maybeRecenter` in the same callback (`WorkingCanvas.tsx`, lines 2161, 2192 and 2197 at cdfebef1).
  - For a fit, case 3, or the DEV seam in run D: the first span line after the first `view-state` line that shows the new origin (lines 1439 to 1448 and 2404 to 2410 at cdfebef1).
- **Long gap:** a kept gap of at least `LONG_GAP_MS`.
- **Baseline:** the median of the same run's other spans whose `totalPositions` is within 5% of the R-span's.

**5.1 The three observations,** each read per run on the 100k fixture.

**O1, the timer.** Read at each R-span of a re-centring drag, case 2's wheel tick, case 3, and run D's re-centring drags and seam calls.
- Confirms candidate 1's shell part: the R-span is at least 100 ms and at least 2.0 times the baseline.
- Kills that part: the R-span is below 1.5 times the baseline.
- Otherwise: neither.
- Recorded beside it: the R-span's share of the kept gap that holds it.

**O2, the counter.** Read at the first metrics window whose page time follows an R-span's t1.
- Confirms the trigger: the window's `updateAttributesCount` (by S0's reading) is at least the R-span's `layerCount`, while the median window with no `layers` line in it shows less than one.
- Kills the trigger: the count is below `layerCount`.
- When the trigger holds and a long gap ends inside that window, the window's `updateAttributesTime` reads one of three ways:
  - at least 50% of that gap: the multiplier is in deck's attribute CPU work;
  - at most 10%: the multiplier is outside that timer (candidate 2 or 3, or deck work the timer does not cover, as S0 records);
  - between: neither.
