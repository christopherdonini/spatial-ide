## §5 Predictions, unchanged items, invalidators, falsification

Written before any run. A wrong prediction is a result; no prediction is ever edited. Terms:
- **R-span:** the span of the render that re-bases.
  - For a drag or a wheel tick: the first span line after the last `view-state` line with the old origin. That line is traced before `maybeRecenter` in the same callback (`WorkingCanvas.tsx`, lines 2161, 2192 and 2197 at cdfebef1).
  - For a fit or case 3: the first span line after the first `view-state` line with the new origin (lines 1439 to 1448 at cdfebef1).
  - Which type applies comes from the step's node mark, never from the line pattern.
- **Long gap:** a kept gap of at least `LONG_GAP_MS`.
- **Baseline:** the median of the same run's other spans whose `totalPositions` is within 5% of the R-span's.

**5.1 The three observations.** Each run, on the 100k fixture:
- **O1, the timer.** Read at each R-span of a re-centring drag, case 2's wheel tick and case 3.
  - Confirms candidate 1's shell part: the R-span is at least 100 ms and at least 2.0 times the baseline.
  - Kills that part: the R-span is under 1.5 times the baseline.
  - Otherwise it is neither.
  - Recorded beside it: the R-span's share of the kept gap that holds it.
- **O2, the counter.** Read at the first metrics window whose page time follows an R-span's t1.
  - Confirms the trigger: the window's layers-updated count (the field S0 names) is at least the R-span's `layerCount`, where the median window with no `layers` line in it shows less than one.
  - Kills the trigger: the count is below `layerCount`.
  - If the trigger holds and a long gap ends inside that window:
    - the window's attribute-update time is at least 50% of that gap: the multiplier is in deck's attribute CPU work;
    - it is at most 10%: the multiplier is outside that timer (candidate 2 or 3, or deck work the timer does not cover, as S0 records);
    - between the two: neither.
- **O3, the line against the gaps.**
  - Confirms: each long gap either holds an R-span with `totalPositions` of at least 1,800,000, or begins within `ADJACENT_MS` after the end of a kept gap that holds one. The latter is the report's ~0.7 s-then-19 s pattern.
  - Kills candidate 1 as a long gap's cause: the gap has no R-span in it and none within `ADJACENT_MS` before it. That gap is listed with its nearest step marks.
- **A run that matches neither.**
  - If a run has no long gap, it shows that on that build, suite and run the re-centre did not stall for `LONG_GAP_MS`. O1 and O2 are still read. The recorded 19 s gaps stay unexplained, since they were seen on a dev build under pan-anchor.
  - If some long gaps confirm and some kill, each gap is read on its own, and candidate 1 holds only for the gaps that confirm.
- **The scale point** (only if OPEN-4 takes one).
  - Under candidates 1 to 3: the R-span and any long gap shrink at least to the 100k values divided by a quarter of the vertex ratio.
  - Under candidate 4 or an unknown cause: a long gap of at least `LONG_GAP_MS` remains.
  - Otherwise neither.
  - Noted before any run: the 2k file lies inside the untiled first look, so it changes more than scale.

## §6 Instruments

All are observations from one machine, not docs/08 measurements. Each instrument and its own cost on what it times:
- **O1 span:**
  - Inside the span, the instrument adds the gate's call and one string compare before t1. That is below the timer's resolution (recorded by the control).
  - The emission comes after t1, in the same task. Its cost is measured and carried on the next line as `prevEmitMs`.
- **O2 window:**
  - Its emission cost is carried the same way.
  - Whether setting `_onMetrics` changes anything in deck beyond calling it is recorded by S0. If it does, the cost is named in S0's amendment before code.
- **Heartbeat:**
  - Tick cost from the control, times 20 ticks per second, gives its share of thread time.
  - No tick runs inside a gap, by construction.
- **Common to every e2e run, not added by this piece:**
  - The devtools client builds previews of existing object-argument lines. This includes the `layers` line inside the span.
  - The measure build's other instrumented-build code.
  - Both are named as confounds.
- **The instruments' share of a long gap:** the sum of the carried emit costs of the lines inside it.
