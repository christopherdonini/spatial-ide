ADR-009 `:43` rules out selling proprietary exceptions, which matches *"without commercial dual-licensing"*. Two readings are the human's, not settled here: (i) whether ADR-009 items 5–6 (proprietary out-of-process plugins permitted; separate commercial services) fall under *"the project may not contain any proprietary, non open-source component"* — they lie outside the signed artifact, but the clause speaks of the project; (ii) whether the installer's WebView2 bootstrapper stubs (`RELEASE-0.1.md:1166`) and a statically linked Microsoft CRT (entry 90) are *"System Libraries"* under GPLv3 §1, the definition `/terms` points to. The three roles meet a one-maintainer repository (DCO enforced by `dco.yml`; the AI agents hold no GitHub identity in the record); whether a single person satisfies *"a team member trusted by the entire team"* is not stated. [unverified] Certum's card-in-reader model fits today's local build and does not fit the hosted runner without a cloud-signing arrangement no fetched page describes. [unverified]
