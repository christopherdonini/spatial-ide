### Finding A2-1
Claim: In the untiled `ViewportStreamManager`, a `viewportQuery` that is still in flight when a source-changed terminal ends the session resolves afterwards and is not refused. Its ticket goes back into the just-cleared `LiveTicketSet`, a stream starts, `requestViewport` returns `issued`, and that ticket's batches are forwarded to `opts.onBatch`.
Suggested severity: S3
Code path (frontends/shell/src/streaming/viewportStreamManager.ts at bb98f71):
- The session-end latch is checked once, at entry, at `:169`. The request takes `myGeneration` at `:185` and awaits `viewportQuery` at `:192`.
- Meanwhile another stream's `onTerminal` runs the source-changed branch at `:305`–`:307`: it sets `sessionEnded` and calls `liveTickets.invalidate()`, but does not bump `this.generation`.
- The post-await guards at `:193` and `:207` compare only generations, so both pass.

- In `sink.onBatch`, the `isLive` check at `:242` passes and `this.opts.onBatch(...)` runs at `:268`.
- In the product, that callback is `makeManagerCallbacks`, which calls `canvas?.pushBatch` at `App.tsx:542` with no session-ended check.
